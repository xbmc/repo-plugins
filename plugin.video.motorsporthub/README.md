# Motorsport Hub — Kodi add-on

Every free and legal motorsport stream in one place, inside Kodi.

- **Live Now** — every official channel streaming this second
- **This Weekend** — upcoming sessions in your own timezone
- **96 official sources** — F1 classics, MotoGP, WEC, IMSA, GT World Challenge, DTM, NASCAR, Formula E, WRC, rallycross, MXGP, karting, drag racing, hill climbs
- **Live TV** — free 24/7 motorsport FAST channels
- **Spoiler-free by default** — titles and thumbnails that give away results stay hidden until you press play

## Install

Download the latest `plugin.video.motorsporthub-*.zip` from
[Releases](https://github.com/Leander86-sketch/motorsport-kodi/releases) and in Kodi:
**Add-ons → Install from zip file**.

YouTube playback requires the official [YouTube add-on](https://github.com/anxdpanic/plugin.video.youtube).

## What this add-on does *not* do

It hosts nothing and re-streams nothing. It links to streams the rights holders
publish themselves on their own official channels, and hands playback to the
official YouTube add-on or the channel's own public HLS feed.
