The library logo identifies Biblioteques Públiques de Catalunya. The original
transparent logo is preserved in library-logo-original.png, recovered from this
project's Git history (commit 752e2c7). The square navy-background adaptation
was prepared with imagegen; library-icon-master.png is its source.
resources/icon.png is the 512×512 opaque RGB export, rebuilt by
scripts/build_artwork.py. The service logo and branding belong to their
respective owners; this project does not claim ownership or relicense them
under the GPL.
